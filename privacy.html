Privacy Policy

Fabrica Video Manager
Last updated: November 16, 2026

1. Information We Collect

The App collects only the information necessary to operate:

  • TikTok account data: profile information (username, profile link) via the user.info.profile scope, used solely to identify the authenticated account.
  • OAuth tokens: access and refresh tokens for TikTok and YouTube, stored locally on your device in encrypted files.
  • Video content: videos you produce are stored locally and uploaded only when you explicitly approve them.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your social media accounts
  • Upload videos you have approved to your connected accounts
  • Display your account status in the management panel

3. Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers other than the social media platforms you choose to upload to (TikTok, YouTube).

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Video content is shared only with the platforms you explicitly choose to upload to.

5. Data Retention

OAuth tokens are stored locally until you revoke access. Video files remain on your device until you delete them.

6. Your Rights

You can revoke the App's access to your TikTok or YouTube account at any time through the respective platform's settings. You can delete all locally stored data by removing the App's data directory.

7. Security

OAuth tokens are stored in local files accessible only to the device owner. No credentials are transmitted to any server other than the official OAuth endpoints of TikTok and YouTube.

8. Changes

We may update this policy at any time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date above.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer through the App's support channels.
